# The 20 questions (English source of truth; Thai/Chinese live in `TXT.th` / `TXT.zh` in quiz.html)

Scale: 1 = "Rarely / Not like me" … 5 = "Consistently / Very true". Each stage scores 5–25.

## 1. Volume — The Creature of Routine

1.1 I go to a social event, class, or group activity most weeks.
1.2 I meet someone new several times a week.
1.3 In the past month, I’ve tried a new group, class, or event.
1.4 I say yes to invitations, even when I’m tired or unsure.
1.5 I start conversations with new people instead of waiting for them to talk to me first.

## 2. Repeated Exposure — The Driveby Friend

2.1 I regularly spend time with the same people through where I live, work, study, or my hobbies.
2.2 I regularly attend the same activity or group, so I see the same people week after week.
2.3 When I meet someone I like, I tend to see them again within a couple of weeks, planned or not.
2.4 After a good first chat, I follow up and suggest meeting again.
2.5 When a plan falls through, I try at least once to reschedule instead of letting it fade.

## 3. Integration — The Compartmentalizer

3.1 With my friends, I do a mix of things (meals, activities, trips), not the same routine every time.
3.2 I introduce my friends to each other and to my wider circle.
3.3 In the past couple of months, I’ve tried something new with a friend.
3.4 I’ve seen my newer friends both in groups and one-on-one.
3.5 My friends know people in other parts of my life, such as my family, coworkers, or other friends.

## 4. Deepen — The “I’m Good” Friend

4.1 I share real things going on in my life, not just surface updates.
4.2 I ask friends for help or support when I need it.
4.3 When someone asks how I am, I answer honestly instead of just saying “good.”
4.4 After hanging out with someone new, I feel like they know as much about me as I know about them.
4.5 When a friend shares something, I ask follow-up questions that go beyond small talk.

## Goal question

What do you want your social life to look like?

A. People to do things with (Coffee, classes, a night out.) → counts stages: Volume, Repeated Exposure
B. A close-knit group (The people I’d invite to my birthday.) → counts stages: Repeated Exposure, Integration, Deepen
C. A few people who really get me (Someone to call after a bad day.) → counts stages: Repeated Exposure, Deepen
D. Not sure yet (A bit of everything.) → counts stages: Volume, Repeated Exposure, Integration, Deepen
E. I’m happy with my social life (Just curious how I’m doing.) → counts stages: Volume, Repeated Exposure, Integration, Deepen
