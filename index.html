How friendships form

Every friendship passes through four steps.

Think of it as a funnel. New people come in at the top, real friends come out the bottom. Most of us leak at one stage more than the others.

1

Volume

The Creature of Routine

Your days are full, just not of new people.

2

Repeated Exposure

The Driveby Friend

You meet lots of people. You just don’t see the same ones often enough for it to stick.

3

Integration

The Compartmentalizer

Your friends live in separate boxes: work, gym, brunch.

4

Deepen

The “I’m Good” Friend

You show up for friends, but rarely let them see the real you.

Yes. No email, no sign-up. You get your result straight away.

Who is this for?

Adult expats and remote workers in Bangkok, whether you landed last month or have been here for years and want a closer circle.

What if I already have friends?

Great! Pick “I’m happy with my social life” in the quiz and you’ll get a snapshot of your strongest stage instead of a leak.
